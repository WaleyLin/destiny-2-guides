## Recommended Loadout (All Encounters)

| Slot | Weapon | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Primary** | Any strong ad clear primary (auto rifle, SMG, pulse rifle) | High fire rate for constant Vex waves across all encounters |
| **Special** | Void Shotgun / Sniper Rifle | Shotgun for close Minotaurs/Praetorians; sniper for range and boss DPS |
| **Heavy** | Rocket Launcher (Gjallarhorn) or Linear Fusion Rifle | Gjallarhorn best-in-slot for boss damage; LFR is a strong solo DPS alternative |
| **Exotic swap** | Xenophage (Machine Gun) | One-shots Oracles; excellent Cyclops/Wyvern clear |
| **Exotic swap** | Witherhoard (Grenade Launcher) | Passive damage on bosses and add-clear zones |
| **Support** | Divinity (Jordan only — Templar & Atheon) | Debuffs boss and creates guaranteed crit bubble for the team |

> **Do NOT use Anti-Barrier mods** — they interfere with the Templar's shield-strip mechanic.

## 1. The Spire

![The Spire](the-spire.png)

**Goal:** Activate and hold all 3 Sync Plates until the Spire forms and the vault door opens.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Any ad clear auto rifle or SMG | Fast TTK on the constant Goblin and Hobgoblin waves flooding each plate |
| **Special** | Void Shotgun | One-burst Minotaurs the moment they step into your plate ring |
| **Heavy** | Xenophage or any Machine Gun | One-shots Cyclops; save your Super for Cyclops emergencies |

> Hold Supers for Cyclops spawns — they are the biggest threat and respawn constantly.

### How it Works
Three Sync Plates spread across the Waking Ruins (left, middle, right). All three must be held simultaneously. **Only Minotaurs can retake a plate.** Cyclops respawn constantly and deal massive damage — kill them the instant they spawn.

### Steps
1. Split into 3 pairs → each pair takes one plate (left / middle / right)
2. Stand inside the ring until the white wall activates — you can then step off
3. Kill Minotaurs before they reach your plate
4. Kill Cyclops with Supers and Heavy the moment they spawn
5. If a plate turns red, one player re-activates it while the other covers
6. Hold until the Spire fully forms

### Player Roles

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | Left Plate — Lead | Calls all Cyclops spawns and Minotaur positions across the encounter; primary kill focus on anything near left plate |
| Jade | Left Plate — Cyclops | Hold elevated angle on left; pop Super or Heavy on every Cyclops the moment Waley calls |
| Jordan | Middle Plate | Stay close to the plate; assist either flank if called by Waley |
| Andre | Middle Plate — Spotter | Take elevated position; relay incoming threats from both flanks to Waley |
| Jebb | Right Plate | Mirror of Jordan; stay on right plate ring; call anything Waley can't see |
| Daniel | Right Plate — Cyclops | Hold elevated angle on right; Super/Heavy on Cyclops priority |

### Tips
- Once a plate activates you don't need to stand on it — use nearby cover
- Middle pair is most central and can briefly assist either flank if Waley calls it
- Don't chase kills too far from your plate

## 2. Confluxes

![Confluxes](confluxes.png)

**Goal:** Defend Vex Conflux towers from enemies trying to sacrifice to them.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Witherhoard (Grenade Launcher) | Stick a shot on Conflux approach paths — passive poison keeps pressure without exposing yourself |
| **Special** | Sniper Rifle OR Void Shotgun | Sniper to pick off Fanatics at range before they explode; shotgun if enemies rush the Conflux |
| **Heavy** | Machine Gun (Xenophage or high-ammo MG) | Wyverns are instant wipe — dump heavy on them the second they're called |

> Fanatics leave poison pools on death — touching them gives **Marked for Negation**. Cleanse at the central glowing pool. **The cleanse pool disappears after one use** — if multiple players are Marked, enter together.

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Confluxes Active | Split |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Phase 1 | 1 (middle) | All 6 together |
| Phase 2 | 2 (left + right) | Waley/Jade/Jordan — Andre/Jebb/Daniel |
| Phase 3 | 3 (left + middle + right) | Waley/Jade — Jordan/Andre — Jebb/Daniel |

### Player Roles (Phase 3)

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | Left Conflux — Lead | Calls all Wyvern spawns for the entire encounter; defends left; coordinates cleanse timing |
| Jade | Left Conflux | Defend left; immediately respond to any Wyvern call from Waley |
| Jordan | Middle Conflux | Defend middle; watch for Marked debuffs and relay to Waley |
| Andre | Middle Conflux | Defend middle; watch for Wyverns on middle lane |
| Jebb | Right Conflux | Defend right; call Wyverns on right side to Waley |
| Daniel | Right Conflux | Defend right; pop Heavy on Wyverns the moment they appear |

### Tips
- Wyverns are **top priority every time** — call them immediately
- Elevated stairs on left/right lanes give good sightlines on approaching enemies
- Between phases, large enemy waves flood the arena — take high ground or survive on cover

## 3. Oracles

![Oracles](oracles.png)

**Goal:** Destroy floating Oracles in the exact order they spawned.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Any ad clear auto rifle or pulse rifle | Kill Hobgoblin snipers between rounds; keep adds off the team |
| **Special** | Sniper Rifle | Long-range Oracle precision kills; Praedyth's Revenge (VoG drop) is ideal |
| **Heavy** | Linear Fusion Rifle or Rocket Launcher | Kills Hobgoblins on floating platforms between rounds; held for Templar afterwards |

### Oracle Locations

```
         [L3]          [R3]
      [L2]                [R2]
         [L1]    [MID]  [R1]
               (spawn)
```

| Label | Position |
|-------|----------|
| L1 | Left side, closest to spawn |
| L2 | Left side, middle height |
| L3 | Left side, furthest back |
| R1 | Right side, closest to spawn |
| R2 | Right side, middle height |
| R3 | Right side, furthest back |
| Mid | Centre of the arena |

### Player Roles

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | R3 + Mid — Lead | Watches R3 and Mid; calls the order sequence for the whole team each round |
| Jade | L1 | Watches L1 only; calls number when it appears in preview; destroys only L1 |
| Jordan | L3 | Watches L3 only; calls number; destroys only L3 |
| Andre | L2 | Watches L2 only; calls number; destroys only L2 |
| Jebb | R2 | Watches R2 only; calls number; destroys only R2 |
| Daniel | R1 | Watches R1 only; calls number; destroys only R1 |

### Steps
1. During the preview, when your Oracle spawns, call its number ("mine's 2nd", "mine's 5th")
2. Waley compiles the full order and calls it out to the team
3. After the preview plays twice, all Oracles appear — destroy in the called order
4. Call out when yours is destroyed so teammates know the count
5. Kill Hobgoblin snipers on floating platforms between rounds

> ⚠️ Oracle audio is often bugged — **always use visual confirmation**, never rely on sound alone.

## 4. Templar

![Templar](templar.png)

**Goal:** Use the Aegis Relic to strip the Templar's shield, then DPS. Repeat until dead.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Any ad clear auto rifle or SMG | Clear Harpies and Minotaurs during DPS setup; keep the area clean |
| **Special** | Void Slug Shotgun | High burst damage on Templar during DPS windows |
| **Heavy** | Rocket Launcher (Gjallarhorn) or Linear Fusion Rifle | Gjallarhorn preferred for pack shot bonus; LFR for sustained damage if no Gjallarhorn |
| **Exotic (pre-DPS)** | Witherhoard | Drop a shot on Templar before Well goes down for free passive tick damage the entire phase |

> Jordan uses **Divinity** the entire encounter — creates a large crit bubble on the Templar making it easier for the whole team to land precision shots.

> **Do NOT use Anti-Barrier mods** — breaks the shield-strip mechanic.

### Aegis Relic Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Fast melee | Melee button |
| Heavy slam (hits Oracles in the air) | Shoot / right trigger |
| Portable shield (blocks all damage) | ADS / L2 / LT |
| Super — removes Templar's shield | Super button |

> ⚠️ If the Relic is dropped, the team wipes after **10 seconds** unless someone picks it up.

### Fight Loop

```
Oracles spawn (3) → kill in order
       ↓
Relic holder fires Super → strips Templar shield
       ↓
All players DPS (aim at crit spot — big red eye/nose; Jordan's Divinity makes this easy)
       ↓
Templar tries to teleport → red circle + beam appears
Relic holder stands in the circle to BLOCK it
       ↓
Repeat
```

### Player Roles

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | Lead — Oracle caller + DPS | Calls Oracle order; coordinates DPS timing; calls teleport attempts |
| Jade | DPS — detained watcher | Full DPS; watches for any teammate inside a detain bubble and shoots them free |
| Jordan | Divinity support + DPS | Locks Divinity on Templar to project crit bubble; adds DPS simultaneously |
| Andre | DPS | Full damage; primary first then heavy during the window |
| Jebb | Add clear + DPS | Clears Harpies and Minotaurs during setup; swaps to full DPS when area is clean |
| Daniel | Relic Holder | Holds Relic entire fight; slams Oracles (ground slam); fires Super to strip shield; stands in teleport circles to block |

### DPS Setup — Corner Method
Set up on the shelf near the Templar's starting position. Place **Ward of Dawn** first (Weapons of Light buff), then drop **Well of Radiance** inside it. Drop Witherhoard on the Templar. Then full burst — slug shotguns, rockets, linears. Jordan keeps Divinity on target the whole window.

### Tips
- Blocking every teleport spawns Praetorians — Jebb handles these so others maintain DPS
- If Templar teleports successfully it resets to back-left, back-right, or mid — Waley calls the new position

## 5. Gorgon's Labyrinth

![Gorgons Labyrinth](gorgons-labyrinth.png)

**Goal:** Reach the exit without being spotted. No combat required.

### Recommended Loadout

> No weapons needed here — there is no fighting. Keep your Templar loadout equipped. If anyone is running **Hunter with Invisibility**, they should scout ahead for the group.

### How it Works
The labyrinth is filled with Gorgons (large glowing Harpies with tentacles). **If any Gorgon spots any player → instant team wipe.**

### Steps
1. Move as a group — do not rush ahead
2. Watch each Gorgon's patrol path before moving through a gap
3. If a Gorgon turns toward you, **stop and wait**
4. Exit is on the **left-hand side** of the maze from spawn
5. *(Optional)* Shoot 3 floating Vex cubes to unlock 2 secret chest rooms

### Player Roles

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | Point — Lead | Leads the group; calls when each gap is safe to move through |
| Jade | Follow | Move only when Waley calls; stay crouched |
| Jordan | Follow | Stay directly behind Jade |
| Andre | Follow | Stay directly behind Jordan |
| Jebb | Follow | Stay directly behind Andre |
| Daniel | Rear guard | Walks last; watches behind the group for Gorgons approaching from the back; calls if anything closes in |

### Tips
- The large rocks on the left of the main area can be used to jump over Gorgons entirely
- **Hunter Invisibility** (if anyone has it) can safely scout ahead before the group moves
- Secret chest rooms stay unlocked even if you wipe after opening them

## 6. Gatekeeper

![Gatekeeper](gatekeeper.png)

**Goal:** Pass the Aegis Relic between portals to kill shielded Praetorians while defending Confluxes inside each portal and Sync Plates outside.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Arc chaining weapon (Riskrunner, Outbreak Perfected, or any Arc auto/SMG with chaining perks) | Arc chains clear groups of Vex inside portals and on the plates efficiently |
| **Special** | Void Shotgun or Fusion Rifle | Close-range burst on Praetorians; Void matches their shields |
| **Heavy** | Machine Gun or Rocket Launcher | Wyverns spawn in the portal without a Praetorian — portal defenders need heavy on them immediately |

> **Key debuff:** After carrying the Relic through a portal you **cannot enter any portal again for ~35 seconds**. This forces the Relic to be relayed between players.

### Encounter Flow

```
Kill central Gatekeeper → pick up Relic
       ↓
Waley + Daniel stand on Sync Plates (left + right) → both portals open
       ↓
Jordan (left portal) + Jebb (right portal) defend Conflux inside
       ↓
Praetorian spawns inside portal → portal defender calls it
Relic holder enters portal → kills Praetorian → drops Relic
Portal defender picks up Relic → exits → hands off to next relay player
       ↓
Relay continues to opposite portal
       ↓
After 3 Praetorians per side → final Conflux spawns in main room → all defend
```

### Player Roles

| Player | Role | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Waley | Left Sync Plate — Lead | Stands on left plate; calls Relic relay timing; kills Overload Minotaurs; kills new Gatekeepers that spawn (they lock portals) |
| Jade | Relic Relay | Carries Relic between portals; hands off to Andre when debuffed |
| Jordan | Left Portal Defender | Defends Conflux inside left portal; calls Praetorian and Wyvern spawns; passes Relic out after use |
| Andre | Relic Relay | Rotates Relic with Jade; carries when Jade has the debuff |
| Jebb | Right Portal Defender | Defends Conflux inside right portal; calls Praetorian and Wyvern spawns; passes Relic out after use |
| Daniel | Right Sync Plate | Stands on right plate; kills Overload Minotaurs; kills new Gatekeepers that spawn |

### Tips
- Wyverns spawn in the portal *without* the active Praetorian — Jordan and Jebb handle them solo with a Super
- Communicate before entering any portal — accidentally going through gives you the debuff
- During the final Conflux phase, Stasis can freeze shielded Praetorians to buy time before the Relic arrives

## 7. Atheon, Time's Conflux

![Atheon](atheon.png)

**Goal:** Survive portal mechanics, destroy Oracles, and DPS Atheon during Time's Vengeance windows.

### Recommended Loadout

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Any ad clear auto rifle or SMG | Kills Supplicant Harpies outside; also handles Oracles inside the portal |
| **Special** | Sniper Rifle | Oracle kills inside the portal at range; available for DPS phase too |
| **Heavy** | Linear Fusion Rifle or Rocket Launcher (Gjallarhorn) | Save all Heavy for Time's Vengeance DPS windows — don't waste it on adds |

> Jordan uses **Divinity** — locks it on Atheon during every DPS window to project a crit bubble for the team.

### How it Works
Each cycle, **3 random players are teleported** into either the Past (left/Mars/red) or Future (right/Venus/green) portal. The inside team destroys 3 waves of Oracles in the order called by the outside team, kills a Praetorian, and exits. Everyone gets **Time's Vengeance** (30 sec damage buff). DPS Atheon. Repeat.

### Oracle Callout Locations
> Outside team calls the order to inside team. **Invert left/right** — outside team's left is inside team's right.

| Label | Location (inside team's POV, facing the exit) |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Spawn / Home | Top of the middle stairs |
| L1 | Left of the top stairs |
| L2 | Close-left, near the portal exit |
| R1 | Right of the top stairs |
| R2 | Close-right, near the portal exit |
| Mid | Centre, closest to outside team |

### Outside Caller Order
When 3 players are teleported in, the remaining 3 outside call the Oracle order. Callers go in this priority order — whoever is outside calls:

**Waley → Daniel → Andre → Jebb**

The first person on that list who is outside takes the call for that cycle.

### Outside Team Steps

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Listen for inside team's call — which portal (left/Mars or right/Venus) |
| 2 | Stand on the correct Sync Plate to open that portal |
| 3 | Active caller watches Oracle spawn locations and calls the order (e.g. "Home, R1, L2") |
| 4 | Repeat callouts for all 3 Oracle waves |
| 5 | Everyone kills Supplicant Harpies; keep 1 floating Harpy alive per side to prevent full respawns |

### Inside Team Steps

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Call which portal you're in ("we're in left/Mars") |
| 2 | One player picks up the Relic — cleanse teammates of Void blindness ASAP |
| 3 | Shoot Oracles in the order the outside caller announces — do this 3 times |
| 4 | Kill the Praetorian near the exit stairs using the Relic |
| 5 | Cleanse team one final time — exit together through the portal |

> Inside team just focuses on shooting — outside caller handles all the Oracle order communication.

### DPS Phase (all 6 players)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Converge on Atheon (center platform or stairs on exit side) |
| 2 | Place **Well of Radiance** + **Ward of Dawn** for damage buffs |
| 3 | Jordan locks Divinity on Atheon — crit bubble makes precision hits easy for everyone |
| 4 | Use burst Supers first (Thundercrash, Nova Bomb, Blade Barrage) — Time's Vengeance regens them fast |
| 5 | **Imminent Detain** — run away from the group immediately; nearest teammate shoots the bubble |
| 6 | When Time's Vengeance ends, return to safe positions and repeat |

### Tips
- Stand on the **pillar in the middle** of the Sync Plate outside — keeps you safe from Supplicant explosions
- Relic holder can briefly drop the Relic during DPS to deal personal damage — pick it back up within 10 seconds
- Keep **1 Harpy alive per side** in the sky outside — prevents the full Harpy group from respawning
- Detained player on the middle platform → jump to the left stairs behind a pillar to isolate the bubble
