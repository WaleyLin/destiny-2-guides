## 1. The Spire

**Goal:** Activate and hold all 3 Sync Plates until the Spire forms and the vault door opens.

### How it Works
Three Sync Plates are spread across the Waking Ruins (left, middle, right). All three must be held simultaneously. **Only Minotaurs can retake a plate** — Goblins and Hobgoblins cannot. Cyclops respawn constantly and deal massive damage.

### Steps
1. Split into 3 pairs → each pair takes one plate (left / middle / right)
2. Stand inside the ring until the white wall activates — you can then step off
3. Kill Minotaurs before they reach your plate
4. Kill Cyclops with Supers and Heavy ammo the moment they spawn
5. If a plate turns red, one player re-activates it while the other covers
6. Hold until the Spire fully forms

### Player Roles

| Role | Players | Job |
|------|---------|-----|
| Left Plate | Waley, Jade | P1 on Minotaurs; P2 on Cyclops with Super/Heavy |
| Middle Plate | Jordan, Andre | P3 stays close to plate; P4 takes elevated angle to spot threats from both sides |
| Right Plate | Jebb, Daniel | Mirror of left pair |

### Tips
- Once a plate activates, you don't need to stand on it — use nearby cover
- Middle pair is most central and can briefly help either flank if needed
- Don't chase kills too far from your plate

## 2. Confluxes

**Goal:** Defend Vex Conflux towers from enemies trying to sacrifice to them.

### How it Works
Three phases, each adding more Confluxes. **4 sacrifices at any one Conflux = team wipe. Any Wyvern sacrifice = instant wipe.** Fanatics leave poison pools on death — touching them gives you **Marked for Negation**, which kills you unless cleansed at the central glowing pool.

> ⚠️ The cleanse pool disappears after **one person** steps in. If multiple players are marked, **enter together**.

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Confluxes Active | Split |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Phase 1 | 1 (middle) | All 6 together |
| Phase 2 | 2 (left + right) | Waley Jade Jordan / Andre Jebb Daniel |
| Phase 3 | 3 (left + middle + right) | Waley Jade / Jordan Andre / Jebb Daniel |

### Player Roles (Phase 3)

| Role | Players | Job |
|------|---------|-----|
| Left Conflux | Waley, Jade | Defend left; call Wyverns immediately |
| Middle Conflux | Jordan Andre | Defend middle; watch for Marked debuffs |
| Right Conflux | Jebb Daniel | Defend right; call Wyverns immediately |

### Tips
- Wyverns are **top priority** every time they spawn — call them loud and fast
- Use elevated stairs on left/right lanes for sightlines on approaching enemies
- Between phases, large enemy waves flood the arena — take high ground or survive

**Challenge — Wait for It:** Let Wyverns begin the sacrifice animation *before* killing them.

## 3. Oracles

**Goal:** Destroy floating Oracles in the exact order they spawned.

### How it Works
5 rounds total (3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 Oracles). Each round shows a preview of the spawn order **twice**, then all Oracles appear at once. Killing them out of order = **team wipe**.

> ⚠️ Oracle audio is often bugged — **always use visual confirmation**, never rely on sound alone.

### Oracle Locations

```
         [L3]          [R3]
      [L2]                [R2]
         [L1]    [MID]  [R1]
               (spawn)
```

| Label | Position |
|-------|----------|
| L1 | Left side, closest to spawn |
| L2 | Left side, middle |
| L3 | Left side, furthest |
| R1 | Right side, closest to spawn |
| R2 | Right side, middle |
| R3 | Right side, furthest |
| Mid | Centre of the arena |

### Player Assignments

| Player | Watches |
|--------|---------|
| Jade | L1 |
| Andre | L2 |
| Jordan | L3 |
| Daniel | R1 |
| Jebb | R2 |
| Waley | R3 **+ Mid** (two locations) |

### Steps
1. During the preview, when your Oracle spawns, call out its number (e.g. "mine's first", "mine's fourth")
2. After the preview plays twice, all Oracles spawn — destroy them in the called order
3. Call out when you destroy yours so teammates know the count
4. Kill Hobgoblin snipers from the floating platforms between rounds

**Challenge — The Only Oracle for You:** Each player may only destroy one Oracle per round.

## 4. Templar

**Goal:** Use the Aegis Relic to strip the Templar's shield, then DPS. Repeat until dead.

### Aegis Relic Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Fast melee | Melee button |
| Heavy slam (hits Oracles in air) | Shoot / right trigger |
| Portable shield (blocks all damage) | ADS / L2 / LT |
| Super — removes Templar's shield | Super button |

> ⚠️ If the Relic is dropped, the team wipes after **10 seconds** unless someone picks it up.

### Fight Loop
```
Oracles spawn (3) → kill in order
       ↓
Relic holder fires Super → strips Templar shield
       ↓
All players DPS (crit = big red eye/nose)
       ↓
Templar tries to teleport (red circle + beam)
Relic holder stands in circle to BLOCK it
       ↓
Repeat
```

### Player Roles

| Role | Player | Job |
|------|--------|-----|
| Relic Holder | Daniel | Holds Relic entire fight; destroys Oracles (ground slam); strips shield; blocks teleports |
| Oracle Watchers | Waley, Jebb | Call Oracle order; help with add clear (Harpies, Minotaurs) |
| DPS + Add Clear | Jade, Andre, Jordan | Full damage on Templar during DPS; one watches for detained teammates and shoots them free |

### DPS Strategy — Corner Method
Set up on the platform/shelf near the Templar's starting position. Place **Ward of Dawn** first (Weapons of Light), then **Well of Radiance** in front. Use precision shotguns at close range. Relic holder stays nearby to block teleports. Jordan on Divinity.

### Tips
- Blocking every teleport spawns **Praetorians** — assign one player to handle them while others DPS
- If the Templar teleports successfully, it resets to 3 locations: back-left, back-right, or mid
- **Do NOT use Anti-Barrier mods** — they break the shield-strip mechanic

**Challenge — Out of Its Way:** Don't let the Templar teleport at all.

## 5. Gorgon's Labyrinth

**Goal:** Reach the exit without being spotted. No combat required.

### How it Works
The labyrinth is filled with Gorgons (large glowing Harpies with tentacles). **If any Gorgon spots any player → entire team wipes instantly.**

### Steps
1. Move as a group — do not rush ahead
2. Watch each Gorgon's patrol path before moving through a section
3. If a Gorgon is facing your direction, **stop and wait**
4. Exit is on the **left-hand side** of the maze from spawn
5. *(Optional)* Shoot 3 floating Vex cubes to unlock 2 secret chest rooms

### Player Roles

| Role | Player | Job |
|------|--------|-----|
| Point | P1 | Leads the group; calls when it's safe to move |
| Everyone else | P2–P5 | Follow directly behind; stay crouched; don't move until called |
| Rear guard | P6 | Watches behind the group for Gorgons approaching from back |

### Tips
- **Hunters with Invisibility** can scout ahead safely — call back what you see before the group moves
- The **large rocks on the left** of the main area can be used to jump over Gorgons entirely
- Secret chest rooms stay open even if you wipe after unlocking them

## 6. Gatekeeper

**Goal:** Pass the Aegis Relic between portals to kill shielded Praetorians while defending Confluxes inside each portal and Sync Plates outside.

### Key Debuff
**Teleport Destabilized** — After carrying the Relic through a portal, you **cannot enter any portal again for ~35 seconds**. This forces the Relic to be relayed between players.

### Encounter Flow
```
Kill central Gatekeeper → pick up Relic
       ↓
2 players stand on Sync Plates (left + right) → both portals open
       ↓
1 player per portal defending the Conflux inside
       ↓
Praetorian spawns inside portal → call it out
Relic holder enters portal → kills Praetorian → drops Relic
Portal defender picks up Relic → exits → drops for next relay player
       ↓
Relay continues to opposite portal
       ↓
After 3 Praetorians per side: final Conflux spawns in main room
Everyone defends it → done
```

### Player Roles

| Role | Players | Job |
|------|---------|-----|
| Plate Defenders (Home Team) | P1, P2 | Stand on left/right Sync Plates; kill Overload Minotaurs; kill new Gatekeepers that spawn (they lock portals) |
| Portal Defenders | P3 (left), P4 (right) | Defend Conflux inside their portal; **call Praetorian spawns immediately**; pass Relic out after use |
| Relic Relay | P5, P6 | Rotate Relic between portals; whoever has no debuff carries it next |

### Tips
- Wyverns spawn in the portal *without* the active Praetorian — solo portal defenders must handle them with a Super
- During the final Conflux phase, **Stasis can freeze shielded Praetorians** to buy time before the Relic arrives
- Communicate before entering any portal — accidentally going through gives you the debuff

**Challenge — Strangers in Time:** Defeat the Praetorian and the Wyvern in the portal at the same time.

## 7. Atheon, Time's Conflux

**Goal:** Survive portal mechanics, destroy Oracles, and DPS Atheon during Time's Vengeance windows.

### How it Works
Each cycle, **3 random players are teleported** into either the Past (left/Mars/red) or Future (right/Venus/green) portal. The inside team destroys 3 waves of Oracles (in the order called by the outside team), kills a Praetorian, and exits. Everyone gets **Time's Vengeance** (30 sec damage buff). DPS Atheon. Repeat.

### Oracle Callout Locations
> Outside team calls the order to inside team. **Invert left/right** — outside team's left is inside team's right.

| Label | Location (from inside team's perspective, facing the exit) |
|-------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Spawn / Home | Top of the middle stairs |
| L1 | Left of the top stairs |
| L2 | Close-left, near the portal exit |
| R1 | Right of the top stairs |
| R2 | Close-right, near the portal exit |
| Mid | Centre, closest to outside team |

### Outside Team (3 players)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Listen for inside team's call — which portal (left/Mars or right/Venus) |
| 2 | Stand on the correct Sync Plate to open that portal |
| 3 | Watch Oracle spawn locations and **call the order** (e.g. "Home, R1, L2") |
| 4 | Repeat callouts for all 3 waves |
| 5 | Kill Supplicant Harpies; keep 1 floating Harpy alive per side to prevent full respawns |

### Inside Team (3 players)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Immediately call which portal you're in ("we're in left/Mars") |
| 2 | One player picks up the Relic — cleanse teammates of Void blindness ASAP |
| 3 | Shoot Oracles in the order the outside team calls — do this 3 times |
| 4 | Kill the Praetorian near the exit stairs using the Relic |
| 5 | Cleanse team one final time — exit together through the portal |

### DPS Phase (all 6 players)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Converge on Atheon (center platform or stairs on exit side) |
| 2 | Place Well of Radiance + Ward of Dawn for buffs |
| 3 | Use one-and-done Supers first (Thundercrash, Nova Bomb, Blade Barrage) — Time's Vengeance regens them fast enough for a second use |
| 4 | **Imminent Detain** debuff player → immediately run away from the group; teammate shoots the bubble |
| 5 | When Time's Vengeance ends, return to safe positions |

### Player Roles

| Role | Players | Job |
|------|---------|-----|
| Outside Team | P1, P2, P3 | Open portal; call Oracle order; manage Supplicants; full DPS during damage phase |
| Inside Team | P4, P5, P6 | P4 holds Relic + cleanses + kills Praetorian; P5 & P6 shoot Oracles; all exit together for DPS |

> 🔄 Atheon teleports **randomly** — who goes inside rotates each cycle. Everyone should know both roles.

### Tips
- Stand on the **pillar in the middle** of the Sync Plate outside — keeps you safe from Supplicant explosions
- Relic holder can **drop the Relic briefly during DPS** to deal personal damage — pick it back up within 10 seconds
- Keep **1 Harpy alive per side** in the sky outside — prevents the full Harpy group from respawning
- Detained player in the middle platform → jump to the left stairs behind a pillar to isolate

**Challenge — Ensemble's Refrain:** Each teleported player may only destroy one Oracle per wave.
